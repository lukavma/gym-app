# Research Notes — Inventory

This directory contains one normalized evidence note per paper in `docs/research/`. Notes follow a fixed structure (Citation, Evidence type, Research question, Population and evidence base, Variables investigated, Main findings, Strength/hypertrophy distinction, Practical interpretation, Application relevance, What this paper DOES support, What this paper DOES NOT support, Limitations, Confidence, Relevant source locations) so that downstream synthesis (`docs/evidence/`) can be built without re-reading the source PDFs.

## Notes on evidence strength going in to synthesis

- Three papers (Pelland, Robinson, and — by definition of being processed as "Refalo") are **preprints, not peer-reviewed**: `pelland-volume-frequency.md` and `robinson-proximity-to-failure.md` are both SportRχiv preprints from overlapping author teams (Zourdos lab), non-pre-registered in Robinson's case. Weight accordingly relative to the peer-reviewed papers.
- `bastos-rir.md`, `korakakis-nippard-training-technique-2024.md`, and `van-every-nippard-load-induced-hypertrophy-2025.md` are non-systematic or scoping-only reviews with no pooled effect sizes — useful for context and directional signal, not for quantitative dose-response claims. **This posture is general, not paper-specific:** it applies equally to any narrative review that enters later, including Spiering et al. 2021 behind the provisional `EVIDENCE-037`, whose maintenance-dose figures are recorded there as context only (2026-09-06).
- `coleman-deload.md` is a single RCT with a disclosed funding conflict of interest (funded by Renaissance Periodization; a co-author co-founded RP) — methodologically solid (preregistered, blinded, Bayesian) but not independently replicated.
- `acsm-2026-resistance-training-position-stand.md` is the broadest umbrella source (137 underlying systematic reviews, >30,000 participants) but reports a vote-count "quality of evidence" (QoE%) metric rather than pooled effect sizes for most granular claims.
- Two independent, non-peer-reviewed meta-regressions on proximity-to-failure and hypertrophy (Robinson) and one peer-reviewed meta-analysis on the same topic (Refalo) **disagree on the shape of the dose-response curve** (continuous/linear vs. trivial/possibly-plateauing) — see `docs/evidence/evidence-registry.md` for the reconciled treatment of this conflict.
- **Three distinct Refalo-led papers exist in this corpus and must not be conflated:** `05_refalo_proximity_failure.pdf` (2023, systematic review + meta-analysis of failure vs. non-failure training across studies), `13_refalo_2024_rir_vs_failure.pdf` (2024, standalone 8-week within-participant RCT directly comparing failure to 1–2 RIR training), and `14_refalo_2024_rir_accuracy.pdf` (2023/2024, standalone randomized crossover trial measuring RIR self-report accuracy on the bench press). All share overlapping authorship (Refalo, Helms, and/or Hamilton/Fyfe); papers 13 and 14 additionally draw on data from the same broader research program's "pretesting" phase but are separate publications with separate research questions.
- `chaves-overload-progression.md` (paper 11) is the corpus's first and only direct intervention evidence comparing load-progression vs. repetition-progression schemes; `baz-valle-training-volume.md` (paper 12) is a small (6–7 study), muscle-specific companion to the larger `pelland-volume-frequency.md` volume evidence, using a different (full-credit, not fractional) set-counting convention — see cross-reference notes in each file before treating their set counts as directly comparable.
